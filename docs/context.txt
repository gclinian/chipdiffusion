## Key learnings / insights to remember

1. **Eval.py path bug**: `from_checkpoint=logs/diffusion_debug/X` gets prefixed
   again with log_dir → double path. Always use relative `X` form. Check eval
   log for "successfully loaded state dict for model" to confirm checkpoint loaded.

2. **Pretrained large-v2.ckpt is at `../public-models/large-v2/large-v2.ckpt`**
   relative to log_dir.

3. **DDPO local reward fails** (3 variants tried) because:
   - predicted_x0 at early timesteps is ill-defined (noisy estimate)
   - policy gradient with batch=2 has too much variance
   - reward magnitude can explode at early timesteps (v2.4 saw 1e10)

4. **Supervised fine-tuning is the workhorse**: Ablation 10k (44.01) beats
   every DDPO and AddLoss variant including 108-min PPO training.

5. **AddLoss (ReFL-style) doesn't beat plain supervised** even with timestep
   weighting. Hypothesis: ground truth placement already has low HPWL, so
   the auxiliary signal is largely redundant. Direct gradient mechanism
   itself is stable (unlike DDPO local reward), just not adding new info.

6. **Professor meeting 0403** (docs/meet/meet_0403.md) suggested:
   - Training loss HPWL/legality (AddLoss direction — tested, marginal)
   - Local reward in DDPO (tested, failed)

7. **Val loss ≠ ISPD performance**. In Ablation 10k, val loss went UP
   (0.10→0.17) from step 1000 to 10000 but ISPD improved. Slight
   overfitting to v1.61 helps ISPD.

8. **Seed variance is significant** (adaptec2: 29.26-36.61 across 4 seeds).
   Multi-seed reporting recommended.

9. **bigblue2 (23k macros) is the only circuit losing to paper**:
   - Our methods: HPWL 57-66
   - Paper: 38.8 (-46%)
   - Reason: V×V matrix for 23k macros needs 30-35 GB VRAM; we have 24 GB
   - skip_guidance_threshold=10000 disables guidance for bigblue2, leaving
     only legalization
   - Paper likely uses larger GPU (40GB+) or cluster-aware guidance

10. **System CPU contention**: Other users (ansys.e, redhawk+) can crater
    eval speed by 5-13x. Check `uptime` and `top` if eval seems abnormally
    slow.
